
# 📊 DecodeLabs E-Commerce Sales Analysis Project4

This project is an E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard built with Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales, orders, products, order status, coupon usage, and referral sources.

## 🎯 Key Highlights

- Total Sales: $1.26M
- Average Sales: 1,053.97
- Total Orders: 1,200
- Chair and Printer are the highest selling products with a total sales of 196K each.
- Lowest-selling product is phone with 152K
- Instagram is the highest referral source with a sales of 275K.
- Cancelled orders: 250
- Returned orders: 247
- Pending orders: 237
- Shipped orders: 235
- Delivered orders: 231

## 🛠️ Tools

Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Excel/CSV
The project demonstrates how raw e-commerce data can be transformed into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that provides clear insights into product performance, customer orders, order status, and marketing channels.

## Business Problems

The analysis identified several areas that may require business attention.

- Sales vary across products, with Phones generating the lowest sales at 152K.
- High cancelled and returned orders: 250 orders were cancelled and 247 were returned, which may indicate issues with product expectations, order processing, or customer satisfaction.
- Pending orders: 237 orders remained pending, potentially affecting customer experience and cash flow.
- Different referral performance: Sales and order volumes vary significantly across referral sources.
Coupon dependency: A significant number of orders were associated with coupon codes, making it important to understand their impact on sales and profitability.

##💡 Recommendations

- Investigate the reasons behind cancelled and returned orders and address recurring issues.
- Review the performance of low selling products and consider better marketing, pricing, or product positioning.
- Improve order processing to reduce the number of pending orders.
- Analyze referral channels based on both sales and order volume and allocate marketing resources based on measurable performance.
- Evaluate coupon campaigns to determine whether discounts are generating incremental sales or simply reducing revenue.
- Monitor the dashboard regularly to identify changes in product, order, and marketing performance and support data-driven business decision.

## Conclusion.

With $1.26M in total sales across 1,200 orders, the analysis highlights both strong performing areas and opportunities for improvement. The insights can help the business improve product performance, reduce cancellations and returns, optimize marketing channels, and make more informed, data driven decisions.
